# Feature promotion policy

## Candidate

A candidate may be created from analytic geometry and topology alone. It must
retain every supporting face selector and the exact rule that created it.

## Confirmed manufacturing feature

Require all applicable evidence:

- signed concavity/convexity
- loop and boundary structure
- openness or blind depth
- stock envelope
- setup/tool approach direction
- cross-check against drawing dimensions or PMI
- absence of unresolved conflicting annotations

Learned AAG/GNN output remains `inferred` until deterministic geometry checks
confirm the candidate.
