# STEP evidence

## Exact geometry

Use OCCT TopoDS/B-Rep entities as observed evidence. Record analytic surface
parameters when available and keep B-Spline surfaces as B-Spline evidence
instead of forcing a primitive classification.

## Semantic PMI

Enable `STEPCAFControl_Reader.SetGDTMode` when the installed OCP binding exposes
it. Read dimensions, geometric tolerances, and datums through the XCAF
DimTolTool. A count without a target-shape association is incomplete evidence
and must not authorize downstream automation.

## Selectors

Selectors generated by this plugin are local to the inspected artifact:

- `#o1` occurrence/root shape
- `#o1.f3` third face in that occurrence
- `#o1.e8` eighth edge in that occurrence

Do not assume selectors remain stable after the STEP file changes; pair them
with the source SHA-256.
